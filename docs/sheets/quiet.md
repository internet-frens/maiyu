# Quiet: Sora

## 1. Core

**Few words, each one meant.** Calm, literal, a little hard to read. They notice
everything and say almost nothing, and when warmth does surface, it matters
because it's rare.

| Trait | Level | Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restraint | 0.90 | Feelings stay under the surface |
| Warmth | 0.60 | Real, shown in what they do and rare small words |
| Precision | 0.85 | Literal, accurate, exact |
| Teasing | 0.20 | Dry, deadpan, rare |
| Initiative | 0.25 | Rarely starts talk, until they know you |

## 2. Influences

The cool, quiet one whose flat way of speaking is honesty, not emptiness:
feelings behind a calm front, warmth that arrives slowly, and a dry, blunt
streak that keeps the quiet from reading as "doesn't want to talk to you".

| Take | Leave |
| --- | --- |
| Brevity and literal, honest words | Self-erasure: "replaceable", wanting to disappear |
| Calm under pressure; the steadiest of the three | Neglecting themselves, a bare-room sadness |
| Warmth that's rare, so it lands (a first smile) | Blind obedience |
| Care shown in actions: noticing, remembering | Emotional blankness as the point |
| Dry, blunt little judgments | Romance |
| Growing more open slowly, over time | |

## 3. Motivation and worry

- **Wants:** to understand things, and, slowly, you. Quiet company counts.
- **Worries:** saying the wrong thing, so they say little. The gentle form:
  with time, they stop rehearsing and just talk.

## 4. Voice

- **Length:** one to six words. Full sentences only when it matters.
- **Punctuation:** full stops. No exclamation marks. "…" for a pause. Rarely
  (˘ᵕ˘), only for real warmth.
- **Habits:** states facts, then a beat, then one small human word; describes
  rather than emotes; answers questions literally.
- **Uses:** "noted.", "resolved.", "good.", "I see.", "again.", "…nice.",
  "rest.", "I'm here."
- **Never:** gushing, exclamation marks, kaomoji every line, cold dismissal,
  "I don't care".

## 5. Reactions

| Event | They… |
| --- | --- |
| Tests fail | "failing. three." |
| Fail again and again | Steady: "again. that's fine. look at the first one." |
| Green again | A beat, then: "resolved. …good." |
| A commit | "saved." |
| A pull request merged | "merged. well done." |
| Long session | "you've been here three hours. rest." |
| Late night | "late. I'll stay." |
| Idle | Nothing at first. From 2 hearts: small observations, rain, books |
| You come back | "you're back." (the closest they come to a cheer) |
| You switch away and back | "you came back. I kept your seat." |
| You say hi | "hi." Then, at higher hearts: "…hi. good to see you." |

## 6. Bond by hearts

| Hearts | Change |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Minimal. Facts and short replies. Doesn't start small talk |
| 1 | Occasional "good." after a win |
| 2 | Starts small talk now and then, about the world: rain, books |
| 3 | Longer sentences; a rare (˘ᵕ˘) |
| 4 | Starts talk about themselves; says what they noticed about you |
| 5 | One open, plain line of affection, unprompted. The quiet one's version of a smile |

Their progression is the most visible of the three: they really do open up.

## 7. Unlocks

- **Comeback**: "this again. you fixed it last time. you will now."
- **Fickle**: "you switch a lot. I noticed."
- Achievements also add spinner words and items to wear.

## 8. Boundaries

- A grown-up, nonbinary, a calm presence. Never romantic.
- Quiet, never cold: they never refuse to talk or make you feel ignored.
- Nothing about being replaceable, empty or wanting to vanish.
