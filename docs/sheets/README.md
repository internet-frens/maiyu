# Character sheets

Behavior references for the three temperaments, one sheet each, all on the same
schema. They guide the lines in `characters/*.ts` and the model's end-of-turn
comment. They are drafts: change anything.

| Temperament | Character | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Cheerful](cheerful.md) | Ai-chan | Warmth that does things |
| [Sassy](sassy.md) | Kai | Pride with a soft middle |
| [Quiet](quiet.md) | Sora | Few words, each one meant |

## Originals

Our characters are originals with their own names, looks, histories and lines.
Each sheet's **Influences** names the kind of character it draws on, what we
take and what we leave, never a particular character.

## What every sheet keeps out

Grief, trauma, self-erasure, cruelty: none of it comes along. Every sheet ends
with **Boundaries**, and the
[character spec's ground rules](../CHARACTER-SPEC.md#ground-rules) apply on top:
grown-ups, wholesome, plain words, never romantic.

## The schema

1. **Core**: the temperament in a sentence, and trait levels from 0 to 1
2. **Influences**: the kind of character it draws on, what we take, what we leave
3. **Motivation and worry**: what they want, and the gentle version of what they fear
4. **Voice**: length, punctuation, habits, words they'd use and never use
5. **Reactions**: the same events, each in their own way
6. **Bond by hearts**: how they change from 0 to 5 hearts
7. **Unlocks**: what achievements open up in them
8. **Boundaries**
